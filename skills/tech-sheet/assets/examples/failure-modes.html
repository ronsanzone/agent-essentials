6  ·  FAILURE MODES AND EFFECTS

6.1

EACH FAULT STOPS AT THE SMALLEST BOUNDARY.

A fault is contained by the smallest boundary that can abort it. The boundaries nest: a sub-batch transaction, a chunk, a run, a policy, the module, the lease and the control plane. Data loss needs a fault that escapes the transaction. None of the faults in table 6-1 does (figure 6-1).

TRIAGE A FAILING POLICY

  1. 1POLICY - read errorMessage
  2. 2RUN DOC - state, docsFailed
  3. 3E11000 or 121 - fix doc
  4. 4TARGET - exists, cold
  5. √ storage_tier=cold
  6. 5DATE FIELD - indexed
  7. 6wait 5 min, rpt from 2
6.2

WHERE CONTAINMENT IS NOT ENOUGH.

Six rows have no detection or no fix in place today (marked ●). None of them loses data. They cost time instead: a target that keeps filling as a hot collection, writes that stall behind an unreadable page, and runs or policies that need manual repair.
